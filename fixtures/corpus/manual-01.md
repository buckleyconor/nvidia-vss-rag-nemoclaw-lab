# M-3021 Motor Drive — Maintenance Manual (excerpt)

## 4.2 Bearing temperature limits

- Normal operating range: 40–60 °C at the bearing housing.
- Sustained temperature above 75 °C indicates bearing degradation.
- Bearing temperature above 75°C: replace per preventive schedule.
- Do not defer replacement when vibration is also elevated (see the
  M-3021 maintenance log entries).

## 4.3 Replacement procedure

1. Lock out / tag out the drive.
2. Replace the bearing per the preventive maintenance schedule
   (schedule-01, item PM-07).
3. Record the replacement in the maintenance log with the part number.
